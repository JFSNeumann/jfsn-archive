Five Decades of Creative Rebellion

From early digital exploration to AI-powered innovation — refusing to play safe, pushing boundaries, and exploring unapologetic creative expression

1984: Digital Pioneer

Pioneering 2D and 3D animation when pixels were revolutionary. Exploring digital art's untapped potential before it became mainstream.

1996: Web Revolution

Embracing the internet's rise with Macromedia Flash, crafting dynamic experiences that merged visual aesthetics with emerging UX principles.

2005: Interactive Era

Transforming static pages into engaging, user-centric digital experiences that anticipated user needs and behaviors.

2011: Mobile-First

Mastering responsive design as smartphones revolutionized how humans interact with digital products.

2022-Now: AI Integration

Leading the charge in AI-powered UX, where machine intelligence enhances human creativity — never replacing the human touch.

Artistic Philosophy: Innovation Through Experimentation

Two forces drive every piece — one ignites, one anchors. My work explores the dynamic tension between creative expression and thoughtful design, finding harmony in contrast.
