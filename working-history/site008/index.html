Why This Site Mattered

This is a snapshot of work in progress. Unlike the polished, completed sites that precede it, this 2020 Bootstrap portfolio was abandoned mid-personalization—the navigation is complete, the content is present, but the customization was never finished. It's a rare archival artifact: not a completed project, but evidence of one. It marks the end of the portfolio-as-active-project era.
