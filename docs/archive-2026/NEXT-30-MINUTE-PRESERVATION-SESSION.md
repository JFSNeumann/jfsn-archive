## Part 3 — One lost work, described *(~15 minutes, optional)*

Pick **one of the large lost pieces you thought were really good** — whichever one you can still see. Six small questions, short answers fine:

1. What kind of thing was it — collage, painting, construction?
2. How big — compared to a door, a couch, a car?
3. What was on it / in it — what would I have seen from six feet away?
4. Roughly when — which decade is fine.
5. Where was it made?
6. What happened to it?

A quote, if one comes, gets kept verbatim. "Don't know" is a recorded answer, not a failure. *(Saying the answers into the same voice memo = recording #3, "One Lost Work I Remember," done simultaneously.)*

*→ Register entry #3 — the first lost work captured since the register was created.*
