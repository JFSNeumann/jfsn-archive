# Design Phase — Reopened

*Supersedes [`STEWARDSHIP-DECLARATION.md`](STEWARDSHIP-DECLARATION.md)'s closure of the design phase.*
*One page. Signed. Dated. Not to be rewritten — only, if ever, succeeded again.*

---

On 2026-07-08, the design phase of the JFSN Archive was declared closed: design would not reopen on opinion or expert critique, only on evidence from real visitor observation or preservation necessity.

On 2026-07-21, Jeffrey F. S. Neumann — the archive's keeper, in that role — directed that this closure be lifted.

**The design phase is open again.** Design decisions are Jeff's to make, on his direction, at any time — the same standing every other decision in this archive already has. No reopening trigger, evidence threshold, or ceremony is required before a design or interaction change may be proposed or implemented. `STEWARDSHIP-DECLARATION.md` stands as the record of the period when that was not true; it is not edited to say otherwise. This page is what governs from 2026-07-21 forward.

Nothing else in the Constitution, the mission, or the archive's non-negotiables changes: archival integrity, honest labeling of composites and estimated dates, and the guiding question — *will this help a future grandchild understand Jeff and his life better?* — still govern every change, design included.

*Not be careful. Be early.*
