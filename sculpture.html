Jeffrey F. S. Neumann · 1974–Present

Sculpture

Extended into space.

76 works · 1974–present

Three-dimensional work has run alongside the collage practice from the beginning. Vacuum-formed plastic and fluorescent tubes from the CIA years. Found objects — the same logic as the flat work, but extended into space. Industrial hardware, repurposed materials, objects that carry their own history before they arrive in the studio. These 76 pieces don't try to be refined; they try to be honest about what materials are and what they've already been.
