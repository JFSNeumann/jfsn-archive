Guernica

232 works · 1990–2020

232 works drawn from and in dialogue with Picasso’s Guernica. Warplanes, targets, fragmented figures, concentric rings, and the visual grammar of conflict rearranged through five decades of collage and assemblage. These pieces are not illustrations of the source painting but a sustained conversation with its underlying logic: what it means to render violence in the language of form. Browse with filters →
