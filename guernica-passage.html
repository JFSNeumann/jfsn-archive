THE GUERNICA PASSAGE

One structure, thirty years.

232 WORKS · 1990 – 2020
↓

Jeff read a book about how Picasso made Guernica. He considered it the most important painting of the twentieth century — the one that showed what painting could do when history demanded something of it.

“Nice and big. Used it as a direction, a structure to work against.” JEFFREY F. S. NEUMANN

That was sometime around 1990. The series ran for thirty years. The composition — warplanes, compressed bodies, fractured geometry — became a scaffold he could build anything on. The materials changed decade by decade: prism paper, compact discs as halos, vinyl, target forms, whatever was available. The structure underneath stayed.

What follows is all 232, in order. Walk.
