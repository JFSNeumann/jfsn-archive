# The Physical Pass — walkthrough sheet

**Last Updated:** 2026-08-03
**For:** Jeff, walking around with a phone recording.
**Why:** Priority 1 of the two remaining in `PROJECT-STATUS-2026.md`. Zero of 1,087 works have
a recorded physical dimension, and no document anywhere records where any of them are.

---

## The setup

1. Voice Memos on the iPhone. Hit record and start walking.
2. One long recording is fine. Several short ones are fine. Stopping halfway is fine.
3. Save as `YYYY-MM-DD-physical-pass.m4a` into:

   ```
   docs/sources/oral-history/audio/
   ```

   *(Note: `SUGGESTED-RECORDINGS.md` says `/docs/oral-history/audio/`. That directory does not
   exist — the `sources/` level was added later and that instruction was never updated. Use the
   path above.)*

4. Tell Claude it's there. Transcription, extraction into the record, and the master-notes entry
   are not your job.

---

## What to say

Six things. In any order. Skip anything that bores you.

**1. Where am I standing?**
Name the place. "This is the hall closet." "This is the basement, north wall." That sentence
alone is the single most valuable thing missing from the archive.

**2. What's in here?**
Roughly. "Maybe two hundred of the big collages." "All the sculptures except three."

**3. How big are these?**
Don't measure. Estimate, and estimate in groups — "these are all about three by four feet,"
"the ones in this stack are half that." If you worked in repeated formats, name the formats.
Four or five sizes probably covers almost everything.

**4. What's underneath?**
What did you glue to. Foam core, cardboard, canvas board, panel, plain paper. If it changed
over the years, say when it changed.

**5. What's holding it together?**
The adhesive, and the tape. 242 works are recorded as containing tape of an unknown type, and
tape is what fails first — it yellows, it lets go, and it takes the surface with it when it
does. Brand names if you remember them, "the red-handled gun" if you don't.

**6. Anything you'd want someone to know before they touch it?**
Fragile, curling, stuck together, don't stack. Whatever you'd say to someone about to pick one
up.

---

## What this is not

Not an inventory. Nobody needs 1,087 measurements. Six or ten group-level answers gets the
archive from *nothing recorded* to *findable and handleable*, which is the whole gap.

Also: this doubles as Priority 4. 34 seconds of recorded voice exist in this archive. Talking
while you walk is the only task on the remaining list that moves two priorities at once, and
recorded voice is the one category that cannot be reconstructed by anyone later.
