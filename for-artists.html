What you get

AI-Generated Catalog

Every work gets a precise, two-sentence description written by Claude — covering composition, palette, materials, and motifs. No writing required from you.

Full-Text Search

Visitors search by keyword, theme, decade, medium, or palette color. Results update instantly. Works on mobile.

Series & Themes

Group your work into recurring themes and named series. Each gets its own page, description, and work count — curated by you.

Timeline View

Works arranged by decade — 1970s through 2020s — in a horizontal strip. A visual biography of your practice.

Constellation

A force-directed galaxy of all your works, clustered by theme. An unusual way to see the shape of a practice.

Open API

A public JSON API exposes your full catalog under CC BY 4.0. Curators, researchers, and collectors can query your archive directly.

Works Offline

Service worker caches the archive for offline use. Visitors at a gallery opening with no signal can still browse your full catalog.

Your Domain

You Own Everything

The site is yours — plain HTML, CSS, and JSON files. No platform lock-in, no monthly fees to me, no login required to view your work.

How it works

You send your images

JPG, HEIC, or PNG — whatever you have. A shared folder (Dropbox, WeTransfer, Google Drive) works fine. No special preparation needed.

We define your vocabulary

A short conversation about your recurring themes, named series, and how you think about your work. This shapes how the AI catalogs everything.

AI catalogs every work

Each image is read by Claude. It writes a precise description, assigns themes, identifies palette and materials, and flags recurring motifs.

You review, I deploy

I send you a preview to review. You request any changes. Once approved, it goes live on your domain — usually within a week of receiving your images.

Questions

What image formats do you accept?

JPG, HEIC (iPhone), and PNG. Any resolution — the system resizes to three sizes automatically (full, thumbnail, mini).

What if I have works without photographs?

That's fine. I can catalog only what you have photographed. The archive can be expanded later as you shoot more work.

Do I need a developer to maintain it?

No. Adding new works is a single shell command (./add-works.sh) that I'll walk you through. Everything else is static — there's nothing to maintain.

How accurate are the AI descriptions?

Very good for visual description — palette, materials, composition. Thematic interpretation is guided by the vocabulary we define together. You review everything before it goes live.

Is this open source?

Yes. The code is MIT licensed on GitHub. A technically inclined artist could fork it for free — the service is for artists who'd rather not deal with the terminal.

How long does it take?

Typically one week from receiving your images to a live preview. Final deployment within a day of your approval.

What about ongoing costs?

Nothing owed to me after setup. Hosting is free (Netlify) or ~$5/month (cPanel). Adding future works costs a few cents each in AI API fees.

What if I have thousands of works?

The system has been tested at 1,084 works with full performance. Contact me to discuss large archives — pricing is negotiable for significant collections.
