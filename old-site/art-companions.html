Student Art Journey

A friendly guide for young artists (ages 5–19) preparing for the Royal Academy Young Artists' Summer Show. Track progress, check size requirements, and write reflections.

  • Free to enter
  • Max size: 95 cm × 300 cm

  • Size guide with visual reference
