Recurring Theme

Framed Pieces

230 works · 1970–2020

Works conceived with framing as an integral compositional element — the frame not as neutral container but as collaborator. Edge and interior in negotiation; the boundary of the work as part of the work itself. Across 230 works, Cleveland artist Jeffrey F. S. Neumann treats the physical frame as a participant in the image rather than a finishing gesture.
