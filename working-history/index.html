Working History Collection

A museum of preserved historical websites. Each record below is an exhibition: the preserved website itself, framed by interpretation about why it mattered and what it reveals about its moment. Click through any record to explore the website and read the story around it.

Nine websites spanning fifty years of creative practice. From a 2000 fine art portfolio to a 2020 professional archive. Each website documents a different strand of Jeff's work — fine art, professional design, family collaboration, and street practice. Together they trace the evolution of digital presentation in a creative life.
