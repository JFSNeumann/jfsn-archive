# FACILITATOR-PACKET.md

*Experience Studio Phase Two · For use during live observation sessions.*
*Use this document during sessions. Refer to VISITOR-OBSERVATION-STUDY.md for full methodology.*

---

## Before You Open the Archive

Write one sentence in the "pre-session prediction" field on the Observation Worksheet: your best guess for this session — will the shift occur, and roughly where. Do not look at it again until you reach the Transformation section of the Post-Session Synthesis Form.

This is not a hypothesis to prove. It exists so that later, when you code what actually happened, you can see how much your expectation was steering your attention.

## Session Setup

Open **jfsn.com/archive.html** in a fresh browser window on a desktop or laptop computer. No other tabs visible. No prior browsing history of the site on this device.

If using a device other than desktop or laptop, record the device type on the Observation Worksheet. Mobile and tablet sessions produce a structurally different experience and should be noted as a separate condition.

Sit to the side or slightly behind the visitor. You should be able to see the screen without being in their sightline.

Have the Observation Worksheet in hand, pen ready. Do not type notes while the session is running.

---

## Opening Script — Say Exactly This, Nothing More

> *"I'm going to ask you to spend some time with this. There's nothing you need to do or find. I'll be quiet while you're in it. I may ask you something afterward."*

Then open the Archive and step back. Say nothing else.

- If the visitor asks what they should do: **"Whatever you'd naturally do."**
- If they ask whether there's something specific to find: **"No."**

---

## Observation Rules

**Do:** Watch pace changes, stillness, and changes in the quality of attention. Note timestamps. Note which elements receive interaction. Remain silent.

**Do not:** Explain the archive. React to what the visitor does. Nod or make sounds. Prompt them to scroll further or stay longer. Correct their navigation.

---

## Pace Anchors — Use These Consistently

- **Fast:** continuous scroll, no pauses
- **Medium:** intermittent scroll with brief hesitations
- **Slow:** frequent stops, sustained attention on individual cards
- **Stopped:** no movement for 10 or more seconds

---

## When Questions Are Permitted

**During a natural pause (30+ seconds of stillness)** — choose one only:

> *"What's happening for you right now?"*

or

> *"What are you seeing?"*

If the visitor falls silent mid-answer: *"Take your time."* Then return to silence.

**When the visitor appears to be finishing** — ask exit questions before they fully disengage.

Do not manufacture question moments. If the visitor moves through the Archive without pausing, do not intervene.

---

## Exit Questions — Ask in Sequence, Stop When an Answer Is Substantive

**1.** *"What did you encounter in there?"* — wait for the full answer.

**2.** *"Was there a moment when it felt different from how it felt at the beginning?"*

**3a.** If yes: *"Can you describe what changed?"*
Then: *"When did that happen — do you remember where you were in the archive?"*

**3b.** If no: *"What did it feel like throughout?"*

**4.** Clear any active filters and scroll to the top of the Archive. Show the visitor the chromatic river. Ask:

> *"What do you make of this?"*

Wait. Do not explain it regardless of their answer.

**Reserve question** — use only once across all sessions, only if a visitor clearly experienced something but cannot find language for it:

> *"If you were describing this archive to someone who couldn't visit it, what would you say it is?"*

Mark the reserve question checkbox on the Post-Session Synthesis Form.

---

## Observation vs. Interpretation — The Essential Distinction

During the session: record only what happened. Not what it might mean.

- **Observation:** "Visitor stopped scrolling for 40 seconds at approximately card 60."
- **Inference:** "The visitor may have entered a receiving state."

Inferences belong in the Post-Session Synthesis Form, not in the Worksheet. If you catch yourself writing what something *means*, write what *happened* instead.

---

## Session Closing

Thank the visitor. Answer any questions they have about the archive now — after their session, not before.

Complete the Observation Worksheet within ten minutes of the visitor leaving.

Complete the Post-Session Synthesis Form within one hour, while the session is still present.
