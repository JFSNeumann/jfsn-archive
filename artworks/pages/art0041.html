Untitled (Sketch, Pink Ball)

Jeffrey F. S. Neumann

Year2000
MediumCollage
SeriesGuernica
ThemesTargets
Palette ivory, pink, yellow, blue, green
Motifscompact-disc, warplane-topdown, american-football, target, concentric-rings, vertical-stripes
Compositionasymmetric horizontal with framed inset left

Collage elements—compact discs, concentric target roundels, a yellow-bordered inset depicting a warplane and pink football—scatter across a white ground with pencil-drawn outlines of vehicles. Along the right margin, vertically stacked iridescent discs and horizontal color-bar stripes anchor the composition.
