Imagined Placements

Gallery Images

149 composites · 1970–2020

Imagined installation views — the work placed, in Photoshop, into gallery and museum settings that were never actually built. These are composites, not exhibition records: most of the work was never physically shown. They picture how the pieces might have hung — an archive of rooms that existed only as images. 149 composite placements by Cleveland artist Jeffrey F. S. Neumann, 1970s to the present.
