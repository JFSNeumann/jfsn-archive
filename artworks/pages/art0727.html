FedEx CD Border

Jeffrey F. S. Neumann

Year2000
MediumCollage
Materialsplastic, paper, tape
Palette white, red, iridescent, black, silver
Motifscompact-disc, perforated-dots
Compositionhorizontal banded rows framing central inset

Rows of compact discs line the top and bottom edges of assembled FedEx shipping envelopes, forming a bordered central panel. Red rectangular elements flank a translucent plastic inset containing printed imagery and layered paper documents; black plastic sheeting anchors the lower edge.
