The 1970s

The first works. Cleveland Institute of Art: prism paper, vacuum-formed plastic, fluorescent tubes, sheets of glass. Rauschenberg on the walls. An early education in permission — the idea that found material was as valid as paint, that anything could be a mark. 58 works from a decade that set the terms for everything that followed.
