Pool Scene on Easel

Jeffrey F. S. Neumann

Year2020
MediumPhotograph
ThemesStudio, Targets
Palette turquoise, orange, yellow, white, green
Motifsbullseye, concentric-rings
Compositioncollage on easel, centrally framed against wall

Mixed-media collage mounted on an easel in what appears to be a studio or warehouse space, with a bright orange warning triangle affixed above it. Central imagery depicts a turquoise swimming pool surrounded by palm trees, a bullseye target, and scattered decorative elements in yellow, blue, and white.
