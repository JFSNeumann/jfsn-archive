Artist, Designer, Creative Technologist

Jeffrey F. S. Neumann (JFSN) has spent over five decades experimenting with form, color, media, and technology.

The work spans analog photography, collage, digital painting, enterprise UX, and AI-driven tooling for large-scale archives. This site serves as both a personal museum and a working studio for new experiments.

The goal is simple: preserve the archive, make it discoverable, and keep exploring.

Early Years

Born February 13, 1955 in Cleveland, Ohio. Received a BFA degree from the Cleveland Institute of Art, 1978.

Artwork Range

Artwork sizes vary from 8"-10" to 24'-14'.

Current Focus

Exploring the intersection of traditional art techniques and modern digital tools, creating visual narratives that challenge conventional boundaries.
