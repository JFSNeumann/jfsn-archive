The 1990s

193 works from the 1990s. The Guernica series begins here: warplanes, targets, concentric rings, the visual grammar of conflict assembled from compact discs, found ephemera, and photographic fragments. The studio practice deepens. Cleveland’s industrial landscape — its particular light and texture — is a recurring presence.
