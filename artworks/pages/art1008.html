Pubiern Robodt 365

Jeffrey F. S. Neumann

Year1990
MediumSculpture
Materialsplastic, resin, paper
ThemesTargets
Palette orange, gold, silver, white, blue
Motifsnumerals, concentric-rings, perforated-dots
Compositioncentral robotic figure flanked by scattered discs

Assembled mechanical components — gears, lenses, circuit boards, and transparent amber casing — form a robot-like figure centered on a white triptych panel. Scattered disc-shaped elements radiate outward across the flanking panels, with red numerals '365' and an inverted red label reading 'PUBIERN ROBODT / ECO-GILD' visible.
