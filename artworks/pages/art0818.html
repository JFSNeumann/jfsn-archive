XXXII Diptych with Targets

Jeffrey F. S. Neumann

Year2000
MediumPhotograph
Materialspaper, paint, cardboard
ThemesFramed, Gallery, Targets
Palette ivory, black, red, yellow, ochre
Motifswarplane-side, bullseye, concentric-rings, chess-piece, american-football, numerals, compact-disc, target
Compositiontwo framed works hung side by side

Gallery photograph of two framed collages displayed side by side on a grey wall. Left panel features warplane silhouettes, bullseye targets, a chess piece, football, and numerals 'XXXII'; right panel emphasizes vertical ochre stripes, compact discs, and concentric-ring targets against a black-and-ivory ground.
