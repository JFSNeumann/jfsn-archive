Privacy

What this site does with your information.

Short version: this is a one-person artist site. Almost nothing is collected. What is collected is only used to reply to you.

What gets collected, and why

If you submit the inquiry form on the For Artists page, the site collects:

  • Your name and email address — so I can reply.
  • The number of works you want to archive, your medium, and any message you choose to write — so the reply is useful.

If you just browse the archive, no personal data is collected by me. There are no cookies, no advertising trackers, no remarketing pixels, and no ads.

Analytics

The site uses GoatCounter for basic, privacy-respecting analytics — pageviews, referrers, country, browser. No cookies are set. IP addresses are not stored. No profiles of individual visitors are built. GoatCounter is open-source and GDPR-compliant by design.

I use this only to understand how people are finding the site and whether the For Artists page is converting inquiries. If you prefer not to be counted, you can enable “Do Not Track” in your browser and GoatCounter will honor it, or block gc.zgo.at in your ad-blocker.

Where the form data goes

Form submissions are routed through Netlify, which hosts this site. Netlify stores the submission so I can read it, and emails me a copy. Netlify’s own privacy notice applies to that transit and storage.

Once I’ve replied (or decided we’re not a fit), the submission stays in Netlify’s form inbox for record-keeping. You can ask me to delete it at any time by emailing jfsneumann@gmail.com.

What I will not do

  • Share, sell, or trade your details with anyone.
  • Add you to a newsletter, mailing list, or marketing automation.
  • Use your message or work for AI training without your explicit, written permission.

If you become a client

If we agree to build an archive together, I’ll receive the images you send and the vocabulary we define for your themes. Those images are stored on my computer and in the GitHub repository for your finished archive. You own everything in that repository; I keep a backup copy so I can help you with future updates, but I’ll delete it on request.

Any testimonial or case-study material from a client engagement will only be published with your explicit written permission.

Logs, hosting, security

Netlify and any CDN in front of it record routine access logs (IP address, timestamp, requested URL) for abuse prevention and uptime. These are not joined with form submissions and are not used to build profiles of visitors.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this notice changes meaningfully, the “Last updated” date below will move and the change will be summarized in a brief note at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or anything else: jfsneumann@gmail.com.
