Paired Framed Collages 1

Jeffrey F. S. Neumann

Year2000
MediumPhotography
ThemesGallery, Targets, Framed
Motifsbullseye, concentric-rings, chess-piece
Palettewhite, grey, yellow, red, black
Compositiontwo framed works side by side

Gallery installation view showing two framed collages hung side by side on a grey wall. Left work features sparse marks, orange rectangles, and bullseye targets on crumpled white ground; right work is denser with yellow, red, black, and white paper fragments, a chess piece, and concentric-ring motifs.
