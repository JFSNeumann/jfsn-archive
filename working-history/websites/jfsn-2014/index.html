About Me

I'm a designer / animator / artist

Here’s My Story!

In 1995, Sun Microsystems introduced Java - and a mascot named "Duke", I created & animated "Wayne" for my first website I designed/developed. In 2002 I started teaching at ITT, classes have included Photoshop, InDesign, Dreamweaver, After Effects, Flash, Illustrator, and 3DS MAX.

I have video broadcast production & post production experience & have worked on 100's of websites over the years.

I recieved a BFA in ID from CIA in 1978.
