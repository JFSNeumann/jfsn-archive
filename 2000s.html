The 2000s

274 works from the 2000s. The collage practice expands. New materials enter: more photography, more three-dimensional assemblage, increasing scale. The archive’s recurring motifs — warplanes, targets, compact discs, torsos, fragments of American vernacular — continue to accumulate and evolve.
