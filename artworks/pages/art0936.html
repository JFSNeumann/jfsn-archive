Cross of Compact Discs

Jeffrey F. S. Neumann

Year1990
MediumCollage
Materialspaper, cardboard, silver-foil, plastic, ribbon, paint, tape
ThemesCrosses
Palette blue, silver, white, ochre, iridescent
Motifscross, compact-disc, vertical-stripes, concentric-rings
Compositioncross form centered on pale ground

Cross-shaped collage constructed from compact discs, cardboard, silver foil, and mixed ephemera arranged on a pale reflective ground with vertical dark stripes. Iridescent CDs flank the horizontal arms while paint, ribbon, and layered plastic elements occupy the central intersection.
