The Rooms

If you want a curated, immersive experience — enter one of these rooms and walk through the work in a particular way.

  • [ THE MUSEUM → ]
    The homepage. Five permanent rooms plus the working studio. Choose your entry.
  • [ THE CURRENT → ]
    Drag through all 1,084 works as a continuous river. Canvas-based. 1970s to 2020s in one gesture.
  • [ THE GUERNICA PASSAGE → ]
    Walk through 232 works built from one obsession. Thirty years of iteration on Picasso's structure.
  • [ THE FLOODED WING → ]
    A room for absence. Works destroyed by water damage, never photographed. Absence as honesty.
  • [ THE HALL OF OPENINGS → ]
    250 imagined exhibitions. Composites, not real shows. Galleries that were imagined, never built.
  • [ THE STUDIO → ]
    The last room. The work in progress. Everything made in the last decade, still being reworked.
