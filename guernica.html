Guernica

232 works · 1990–2020

The Guernica series spans five decades and 232 works — the longest-running named series in the archive. Named for Picasso's 1937 painting, it builds its own visual language: compact discs as warplane halos, target forms as compositional anchors, aerial geometry refracted through collage and assemblage. Conflict without declaration; formation without command. Cleveland artist Jeffrey F. S. Neumann has been extending this series since 1990. All series & themes →
