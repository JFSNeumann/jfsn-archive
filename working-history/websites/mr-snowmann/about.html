About Me

"Believe it or not, I can actually draw."

-- JEAN-MICHEL BASQUIAT

 I was born February 13, 1955 in Cleveland, Ohio and received a BFA degree from the Cleveland Institute of Art, 1978.  Artwork sizes vary from 8"-10" to 24'-14'.

 

This artwork has never been seen by the public before and the large works are made in pieces for easy shipping.

 

I would guess that 2/3 of all the pieces are double sided and that there are about 100 "New" pieces that I have been working on over the last 10 years.
