Three Websites, One Epic Creative Journey

A grandfather's gift celebrating Sebastian's creative evolution—from childhood builds to fine art mastery.

These three websites document Sebastian's creative journey across different phases of his artistic development. Each version captures a distinct period of exploration, growth, and creative expression.

V1

Legos & K'nex

The foundation years—building, creating, and exploring three-dimensional space through construction toys. Early experiments in form, structure, and imagination.
V2

Dragons

Mythical creatures and fantasy worlds. A period of narrative exploration through drawing and character design, bringing imagination to life on paper.
V3

Fine Art

The evolution continues—exploring traditional and digital media, developing technique, and creating works that reflect a mature artistic vision.

A Family Archive

This project serves as both a celebration of Sebastian's creativity and a family archive. Each website captures a moment in time, preserving the evolution of an artist's journey from childhood through young adulthood.

As a grandfather, documenting this creative journey has been a labor of love—a way to honor Sebastian's imagination, dedication, and artistic growth. These websites stand as a testament to the power of creative expression and the importance of preserving family creative history.
