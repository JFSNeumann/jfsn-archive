Targets, Discs, and Planes

Jeffrey F. S. Neumann

Year2020
MediumCollage
Materialspaper, tape, ribbon, paint, ink, cardboard
ThemesTargets
Palette white, red, black, orange, blue
Motifscompact-disc, target, concentric-rings, bullseye, warplane-topdown, warplane-side, american-football, chess-piece
Compositioncentral framed panel flanked by dense layered zones

Large-format collage integrating compact discs, concentric target rings, top-down warplane silhouettes, and a central framed drawing panel with orange flower motifs and geometric symbols. Red ribbon stripes, blue tape, a chess pawn, and an american football appear among densely layered paper and mixed-media elements.
