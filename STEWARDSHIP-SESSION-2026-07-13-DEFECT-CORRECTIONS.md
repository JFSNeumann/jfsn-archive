## Preservation Stewardship Transition

Effective 2026-07-13, this project transitions from active implementation to
**Preservation Stewardship**:

- **The Museum is considered architecturally complete.** Design work is closed.
- **The burden of proof has shifted from "improve" to "preserve."** Future
  changes require objective, independently verifiable evidence of a defect.
- **No UX reviews, redesigns, or enhancement proposals** are to be initiated.
- The archive is now maintained as a **conserved work**, not an evolving design.

Future work is limited to **factual preservation**: metadata accuracy, creator
testimony, oral history, provenance, exhibition verification, lost-works
documentation, historical context, and archival preservation.
