Painting

Present since the beginning.

42 works · 1974–present

Painting appears less frequently than collage or photography in this archive, but it's been present since the beginning — sometimes as ground, sometimes as the whole thing. Works on canvas and panel, often sharing space with found material or photographic elements. The boundary between painting and collage has never been clean, and these 42 pieces sit at different points along that line.
