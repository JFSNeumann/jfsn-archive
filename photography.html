Photography

328 works · 1974–present

Photography in this archive is rarely separate from the other work — images become source material for collage, documentation turns into its own object, the studio becomes the subject. But the photographs also stand alone: a particular quality of Cleveland light, industrial texture, vernacular surfaces that keep reappearing whether they're invited or not. These 328 works span from early black-and-white through color film and into digital, across five decades of looking. Browse with filters →
