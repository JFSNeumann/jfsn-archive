Untitled (Planes, Discs, Chess)

Jeffrey F. S. Neumann

Year1990s (est.)
MediumCollage
OrientationVertical
ThemesTargets
Motifswarplane-topdown, compact-disc, chess-piece, concentric-rings, target, roundel, ribbon-stripe, american-football, perforated-dots
Palettewhite, black, red, iridescent, yellow

Dense mixed-media collage combining multiple top-down warplane silhouettes, compact discs, chess pieces, concentric-ring targets, and ribbon stripes across a white ground. Iridescent foil strips, red diagonal bands, and scattered roundels fill the picture plane in layered vertical arrangement.
