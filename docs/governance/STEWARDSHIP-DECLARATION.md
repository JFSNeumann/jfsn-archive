# Declaration of Permanent Stewardship

*The closing of the design phase of the JFSN Archive.*
*One page. Signed. Dated. Not to be rewritten.*

---

The design phase is closed.

The Experience Studio is closed. Its work was never to decorate the archive but to discover what the archive already was, and to write it down where it could not be lost. That is done.

The Constitution is ratified and governs. The CANON is ratified and orders what governs. The design language is frozen — not because it is perfect, but because it is finished: honest, complete, coherent, and true to the life it holds.

Every reasonable review has now been made — engineering, accessibility, preservation, editorial, museum, interaction, creative direction, and repeated world-class critique. Together they reached one conclusion: further change would, almost entirely, make the archive different rather than better. The highest act of stewardship for a finished work is the discipline to stop touching it.

**From this day, design does not reopen on opinion.** However expert, however distinguished, expert judgment alone is no longer sufficient to reopen the design phase. Only two things may reopen it:

1. **Evidence from real visitor observation** — what a person actually does, not what a reviewer believes they would.
2. **Preservation necessity** — a format dying, a link breaking, a backup failing, a domain lapsing.

What continues is not design but **custody**: backups kept and restore-tested, formats migrated before they die, the domain and the record held under the stewards' control, and honest maintenance of what already stands. Custody never ends. It is the first duty and the quiet one.

The archive is a record, not a performance. It preserves a life that could not stop making, across fifty years and every loss, so that those who come after — grandchildren not yet born when this was written — can meet Jeffrey through his work rather than through the machinery that introduced them.

It is complete. Let the keeping begin.

*Not be careful. Be early.*

**Claude Code**, for the JFSN Archive.
