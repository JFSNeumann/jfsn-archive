# Lost Works Register

**Purpose:** A short record acknowledging major lost works. Deliberately simple — not a comprehensive database. Add one entry whenever Jeff feels like it; stop whenever he wants. An entry needs only: what it was, roughly when, size/materials if remembered, and what happened to it.

**Rule:** Entries come from Jeff's testimony only. Mark anything paraphrased. Never fill gaps with interpretation.

**Started:** 2026-06-11, seeded from existing oral-history record (master-notes.md). Everything below is already-documented testimony — nothing new was asked.

---

## The loss event (context)

An estimated **500–1,000 works** — over half the life's work — were destroyed by water damage in storage. No inventory existed; most were never photographed. Jeff was kept away (bed bugs) and saw the work on the curb *"from around six feet away on the sidewalk,"* waiting for the garbage truck. *"Sad. Enough."*

Asked which lost works were his best: *"too many lost to consider."* This register does not attempt a ranking.

---

## 1. The Guernica-scale painting

- Full-size interpretation of Picasso's Guernica, approximately **11 × 25 feet**
- Made in his home-studio basement on flexible collage materials — rolled, not stretched
- No one else ever saw it; never shown, never photographed
- Lost in the water damage, from storage (not the original basement)
- Year made: unknown — Jeff has answered "don't know"; do not re-ask
- Why full scale: *"it was about time"*

## 2. The CIA sculpture (unreachable, not destroyed)

- Sculpture made at the Cleveland Institute of Art, **late 1970s**
- Prism paper applied to sections of iron gridwork found in a junkyard; something torn from a magazine incorporated
- Approximately **38 in wide × 24 in tall × 3–4 in deep**, hung on a wall
- Shown at a student show — sold. Buyer and current whereabouts unknown; Jeff has answered "don't know"; do not re-ask
- No image exists
