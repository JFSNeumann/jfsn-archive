Mr. SNOWmann

72 works · 1990–2020

A recurring figure — part totem, part pop effigy — assembled from the residue of consumer culture. Across more than 72 works spanning five decades, the Snowmann shape-shifts and accumulates: a sustained riff on American iconography, assembly, and the act of making something from nothing.
