The Museum
That Never Existed.

A personal record, not a gallery.

For decades he made pictures of museums showing his work — walls, crowds, openings that never happened. Every one of them is flagged in this archive as imagined. This building is the one that exists: 1,084 works, fifty years, kept and described and open.
