Contract Administration Tracking System

An integrated case management system with configurable business processes supporting all PBCA processing activities including work plan management, task processing, and communication and document management.










CATS enables PBCAs to increase operational efficiency and maintain compliance. With CATS, PBCAs optimize efficiencies through automation, reducing administrative burden and enabling PHA staff to focus on the needs of tenants and owners.
