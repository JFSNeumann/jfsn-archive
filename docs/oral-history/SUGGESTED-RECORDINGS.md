# Suggested Works for Oral History Recordings

Record 1-minute thoughts on each. Focus on: context, material choices, why it matters, surprises.

## Tier 1: Iconic/Largest (5 works)

1. **art0XXX — Guernica interpretations (series)**
   - "Why did you keep returning to Guernica? What changed in your interpretation?"
   - Scope: 50+ years of engagement with one theme

2. **Art involving lost storage piece**
   - "You mentioned a full-scale Guernica in the basement. What do you remember about making it?"
   - Context: Connects to lost-works register

3. **First digital/photo work in archive**
   - "When did you start incorporating photography? What drew you to mixing mediums?"
   - Scope: Evolution of practice

4. **Largest sculpture in archive**
   - "Tell me about the scale decisions on this. What's different about making big?"
   - Physical presence

5. **Most recent work (2024-2025)**
   - "What are you working on now? How has your practice evolved?"
   - Current context

## Tier 2: Thematic depth (5 works)

6. **Art from "Torsos & Faces" series**
   - "The human figure appears across decades. What's the through-line?"
   - Recurring theme

7. **Collaboration work**
   - "Tell me about working with your grandchildren. How does that change the work?"
   - Family/teaching angle

8. **Crosses or Snowmann series**
   - "Where did these symbols come from? Do they mean the same thing now?"
   - Symbol evolution

9. **A work using found/recycled materials**
   - "What's your relationship to re-using materials? Sustainability? Play?"
   - Process/ethics

10. **A failure or experimental piece**
    - "Not everything works. Tell me about one that didn't or surprised you."
    - Honesty/learning

---

## Recording template (1 min = ~150 words)

```
[Work title/ID]
[Your thought on the work]
[Why it matters / What surprised you]
[How it connects to your broader practice]
```

---

## Setup

1. Use voice memo app on iPhone 15 Pro (built-in)
2. Name: `art0XXX-record.m4a`
3. Save to `/docs/oral-history/audio/`
4. Add entry to `/docs/oral-history/master-notes.md` with:
   - Work ID + title
   - Quote or summary
   - Date recorded
   - Link to audio file

---

## Why this matters

- **Preservation**: Your voice + context = irreplaceable archive documentation
- **Accessibility**: Audio + text helps different learning styles
- **Narrative**: 10 recordings over 50 years tells a story of practice evolution
- **Personal**: This is the archive's soul—the maker's presence

**Start with one recording. Use it as practice. Then batch the rest.**
