What this site does with your information.

Short version: this is a one-person artist site. Almost nothing is collected. What is collected is only used to reply to you.

Updated 1 Aug 2026: corrected the Analytics section below to reflect GoatCounter, used on artwork pages.

What gets collected

Nothing. If you browse the archive, no personal data is collected. There are no cookies, no advertising trackers, no remarketing pixels, and no ads.

Analytics

Artwork pages use GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics tool, to record aggregate page views, referrer, browser, and country — no cookies, no fingerprinting, no personal data, and no cross-site tracking. It does not identify individual visitors. No Google Analytics or ad-network analytics.

Logs, hosting, security

HostGator and any CDN in front of it record routine access logs (IP address, timestamp, requested URL) for abuse prevention and uptime. These are not joined with form submissions and are not used to build profiles of visitors.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this notice changes meaningfully, the "Last updated" date below will move and the change will be summarized in a brief note at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or anything else: jeff@jfsn.com.
