# Creator Voice Plan — Three One-Minute Recordings

**Created:** 2026-06-11 · Jeff's own #1 preservation priority (master-notes §25). No audio of Jeff exists anywhere in the archive — the entire oral history is typed. One minute of voice is the cheapest irreplaceable object the archive lacks.

## The whole plan

Open **Voice Memos** on the iPhone. Press record. Talk for about a minute. Press stop. Done.

- **One take.** No re-records unless *you* want one. Stumbles, pauses, and "uh"s are the point — they're you.
- **No script.** If you blank, each topic below has one starter line — read it aloud and keep going, or ignore it.
- **No production.** Kitchen table is a fine studio. Background noise is period detail.
- **A minute is plenty.** Thirty seconds counts. Five minutes also fine. Stop when you're done, not when it's "complete."
- **Any one of the three is a win.** All three in one sitting, or one per month — both correct.

## The three recordings

**1. Why I Make Things**
*Starter, if useful:* "I make things because…"
(You've answered pieces of this in writing — gut feeling, things that still had a life left in them. Just say it out loud this time, however it comes.)

**2. What I Hope My Grandchildren Understand**
*Starter:* "Sebastian, Caspar, Anthony, Emilia — what I want you to know is…"
(You once wrote: "that they would be very impressed if they had the chance to see all the works in one space." Saying it to them in your own voice is a different object entirely.)

**3. One Lost Work I Remember**
*Starter:* "There was one piece…"
(Any lost work, your pick. What it looked like, how big, what was on it. This recording doubles as a lost-works register entry — one minute, two preservation wins.)

…logged in master-notes (date, duration, topic — content never paraphrased; the recording IS the record), and carried into all four backup stores by the normal end-session run. Nothing gets published on the site unless Jeff asks.

## Rules

- "Next" / "boring" / "not now" ends it, instantly, no follow-up.
- Never re-record to "improve" one. First takes are primary sources.
- These three topics are the whole project. There is no recording #4 unless Jeff invents it.
