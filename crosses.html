Crosses

69 works · 1970–2020

The cross reduced to its geometry — symmetry, perpendicular meeting, four directions held in tension. Form that carries cultural weight before any specific reading is applied, returned to pure structure and asked to hold whatever the viewer brings. 69 works across five decades by Cleveland artist Jeffrey F. S. Neumann in collage, painting, and mixed-media assemblage.
