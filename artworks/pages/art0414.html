Snowmann and the CDs

Jeffrey F. S. Neumann

Year2010s (est.)
MediumCollage
OrientationVertical
ThemesMr. Snowmann
Motifscompact-disc, warplane-side, concentric-rings, perforated-dots
Palettegrey, white, black, silver
Compositionvertical split with snowman left, CDs right
Archive No.ART0414

Monochromatic collage combining a large snowman figure rendered in white outline on the left with a vertical procession of smaller snowman pairs on the right. Compact discs are distributed throughout, alongside warplane silhouettes, photographic textures, and layered paper fragments.

Most catalog text in this archive was generated by software from photographs of the works — not written by Jeff, and sometimes wrong. His own words are in Stories.
